Veri-Gate V0.8 Walkthrough

This walkthrough shows the scanner flow, temporary authorization, Supervisor pages, Search, Feedback, and prototype boundaries.

Step 1 of 12

The Scanner

A gate operator opens Veri-Gate on the handheld. On a phone it shows the scanner and nothing else.

Step 2 of 12

Step One: The Driver

The operator scans or types the driver employee number.

Step 3 of 12

A Vehicle We Have Never Seen

An unknown barcode is accepted. The operator is not interrupted.

Step 4 of 12

Step Three: Direction

The operator chooses Vehicle IN or Vehicle OUT.

Step 5 of 12

Straight Back to Work

After submitting, the scanner returns immediately to the home screen.

Step 6 of 12

The Rule That Matters

The same vehicle is refused on the way OUT.

Step 7 of 12

The Supervisor Console

On a computer, the same build shows the full console.

Step 8 of 12

Incomplete Inventory

Vehicles added by an inbound scan appear in a queue for completion.

Step 9 of 12

Completing the Record

The supervisor fills in the vehicle details.

Step 10 of 12

Released

With the record complete, the vehicle is allowed OUT.

Step 11 of 12

Fleet Lead And Above

An under-ranked approver cannot authorise a blocked OUT.

Step 12 of 12

Search And History

Movements remain searchable by driver, vehicle, plate, VIN, location and date.
