## Defects caught while building the videos

**Three frames contradicted their own narration.** All three were found by checking each captured image against its script rather than trusting the capture code.

1. The Fleet Lead slide used driver `E1005`, whose licence is expired in seed data. The expired-licence check fires before the override panel, so the frame showed a licence error while the narration described a role refusal. Switched to `E1003` — unauthorized but current. The frame now reads *"S3090 / Casey Rowe holds Scanner and cannot approve a Vehicle OUT override."*
2. The customer slide titled *The Supervisor Console* was showing the **scanner** on a desktop. It now opens the console.
3. The video header still read **"Lot Watch / GateFlow V0.7"** — stale branding on every frame of a client video.

**Two presentation bugs.** Desktop slide titles overflowed the canvas and lost their last characters (reported on slide 7); the screenshot is narrower and the text block moved left. Slide timing only ever scaled **up**, so the customer deck ran 5:25 against 4:16 of narration — over a minute of silence; it now matches the audio in both directions.

**A broken link before it shipped.** `.vercelignore` excluded all of `docs/`, which would have made both walkthroughs 404 for Patrick after deployment.
