4. What changed after your September 13 email

All of this is in the app above and in the Android file, but it is newer than the videos, so it is written out here. The manual covers each one in full.

  • Employee numbers no longer show during a scan. A scanned badge draws as dots. The review step, the blocked screen and the saved notice name the person instead. Tap a field to type and the dots come off, because the operator has to see what he is keying in.
  • Active Driver Authorizations now explains itself. It reads, for example, "3 authorized now. 2 active drivers are not authorized today." The panel was never miscounting — Active on the roster means a driver may work, not that he is authorized today.
  • Search shows 50 rows at a time, with a Show next 50 button, and it prints. Put your name in the printouts field first; the printout carries your search, when it ran, how many rows printed, and who printed it and when. The same dialog saves it as a PDF on your own computer.
  • Every date reads MM/DD/YY — six digits, with the time after it.
  • An Admin tab with a per-location override switch, all off by default. With one on, a Vehicle OUT that would normally wait for a Fleet Lead goes through when the badge was scanned, the driver is active, the license is not expired, and nobody revoked him today. It saves as Location override, never as Authorized, and the audit trail records it.
  • Gone: the scanner's Gate activity list, and the Supervisor Locations and Label Printer sections. Drivers and Vehicles are now sorted by most recent gate activity, and the license expiry panel uses the same short labels as the roster.

The power button under App Lock is not in here. Android does not let an app power the phone off — that is a setting on the device, for Samsung or Verizon.

5. What changed after your September 13 call

The call added a few things the email had not. These are newer than the videos.

  • One Actions menu per driver row, in place of the three-line block of buttons — Edit, Mark inactive, Authorize, Revoke. The name still opens the profile. With thousands of drivers the list stays one line each. Anything that changes a driver asks "are you sure" first; tell us if that is one click too many.
  • Active Driver Authorizations is one line per driver — name, employee number, duration, expiry, Revoke. The scope is said once above the table instead of repeating "All current locations" on every row.
  • Save a new vehicle and it offers to print the label. The pop-up shows the label; Print label prints only the label, on a 4 × 2 inch page. Printing is recorded in the history. The label carries the barcode, the number in large type, the VIN, and year, make, model and colour when we know them.
  • The keyboard no longer covers what the operator is typing. The app now shrinks above the keyboard and keeps the open field in view.
  • The date box will not take a bad year. 2020 to 2099 only. A half-typed date stops the search with a message instead of quietly searching every date.

The manager button next to Search is already there — it is the Admin tab, with the per-location override switches.

6. The shared database is running

This is the part you cannot see on the screen, so it is written out here. Up to now every phone kept its own records. That is now finished.

  • Every phone and every console share one set of records. A movement scanned at one gate is in the log for everyone, and a driver authorized in the console reaches the phones within about five minutes.
  • No signal is not a problem. The phone keeps working exactly as it does now and holds everything it recorded. When signal comes back it sends it, in the order it happened. A phone can be out of coverage for days without losing a scan.
  • Everyone signs in, and the record says who did it. Each gate phone has its own login that you set up once; the console asks the supervisor to sign in. Changes are recorded under the person's name instead of "Supervisor Console".
  • The system decides what each person may do, not the app on the screen. A gate phone can scan and record. A Fleet Lead can also search the log and authorize drivers. A Supervisor can also edit drivers and vehicles. Only an Admin touches the override switches and the logins. Changing this on a phone changes nothing — the server refuses it.
  • You add and remove logins yourself, from a Users tab in the console. It gives you a one-time password to hand over; the person picks their own at first sign-in. Turning a login off cuts that person off on every device.
  • The records are kept even if a phone is lost or wiped. They are in the database, not on the phone.

It is running in the Veri-Gate development account today, which is where we prove it before it moves to the live account. Nothing about how it works changes in that move.
